About me

Hi, I’m Laura.

Licensed psychologist with a degree from PUC-Campinas and postgraduate specializations from PUC-RS in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy and in Neuroscience, Education and Child Development. Over 3,000 sessions conducted, focusing on relationships and self-esteem, always with a welcoming approach and goals set together with you.

Learn more

I work with Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), which starts from a practical idea: what we think about a situation influences how we feel and act. In therapy, we identify these cycles together and try out new ways of thinking and acting in everyday life. It is an evidence-based, collaborative approach with clear goals.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

Fees are shared directly in the first contact on WhatsApp. The Brazilian Psychologists’ Code of Ethics does not allow prices to be advertised.

How does online therapy work?

The 50-minute sessions take place by video call, on Google Meet, Microsoft Teams or WhatsApp. All you need is a private space, headphones and a stable internet connection.

I live outside Brazil. Can I have therapy with you?

Yes. I see clients living abroad, and sessions are scheduled according to your time zone.

Do you provide receipts for reimbursement?

Yes. I issue receipts so you can request reimbursement from your health insurance, according to your plan’s rules.
