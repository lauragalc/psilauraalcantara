My approach

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, in plain words

CBT starts from a practical idea: what we think about a situation shapes how we feel and what we do. And what we do, in turn, reinforces those thoughts.

In therapy we spot these cycles together, understand where they come from, and try out new ways of thinking and acting in everyday life. It is an evidence-based, collaborative approach with clear goals.

FAQ

How much does a session cost?

Fees are shared directly when you first message me on WhatsApp. The Brazilian psychologists' code of ethics does not allow advertising prices.

How long is each session?

50 minutes. Sessions are weekly and arranged as a monthly plan of 4 sessions, or 5 in months with five weeks.

How does online therapy work?

We meet over video on Google Meet, Microsoft Teams or WhatsApp. All you need is a private space, headphones and a stable internet connection.

What time zones do you work with?

I work with clients in different countries. We'll find a time that fits both your schedule and mine.

Can I get a receipt for insurance reimbursement?

Yes. I issue receipts you can submit to your health insurance, subject to your plan's rules.

Is what I say confidential?

Yes. Confidentiality is an ethical duty for psychologists and covers everything we talk about in session.
